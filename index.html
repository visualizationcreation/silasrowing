What actually makes a rower faster

Most rowing advice is a list of exercises. This is the reasoning underneath one — worked through in order, from the principle that governs transfer, through the stroke itself, the engine, the lifts, and the two injuries that end seasons, to the week that has to hold all of it. Every lesson carries a concrete prescription, the named strategy behind it, and the evidence it rests on.

Move with Next, the arrow keys, or by clicking any point on the orb.

Each lesson has three parts: the reading (what is true), the practice (what to do about it), and the evidence (where it comes from, with links).

Where the science is genuinely unsettled the lesson says so and is marked as a frontier — those are three of the thirty-three.

Nothing here is medical advice. Pain that persists is a clinician's question.
